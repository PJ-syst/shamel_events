The people behind the moments

A family tradition of hospitality that grew into one of the region's most trusted event houses.

Since 2024

It began at a family table

Shamel Events started in a family kitchen famous among friends for its dinners. When those dinners were asked for at weddings, engagements, and anniversaries, we discovered a gift: we don't just feed people — we make gatherings feel effortless and warm.

Two years later, we've had the honor of producing celebrations of every size across Uganda. The scale changed. The care didn't. Every event still gets a planner who knows every guest's name, a chef who tastes every plate, and a director who sweats every minute of the run sheet.

What We Believe
